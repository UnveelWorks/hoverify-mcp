# Design recipes

Eight worked examples for `design_screenshot`, made on tryhoverify.com. They're starting points, not templates: change anything, and look at the preview after each call.

## How these were designed

- **Headline first.** One heavy line (black weight, line height about 1, tracking a little tight) carries the image; everything else is smaller and quieter.
- **One accent.** Take the brand colours from `get_colors`. The brand colour leads; a second hue from the palette (or a neighbour on the colour wheel) can glow beside it.
- **Make the background a piece of art.** A flat gradient reads as a template. Build it in `art` from a few layers: a base tone, one or two glows in screen blend, a texture that fades (a grid, dots, rings or lines at 5 to 20 % opacity with `fade`), and grain at 6 to 14 %. Give each design one idea: light rays, a colour slab, a split stage, giant type.
- **Light the shot from behind.** Art draws under the shots, so a glow or slab placed where the shot sits rims it with colour. The result says where the shot landed (`The shot spans x … y …`); lay the art out around that.
- **A grid.** Line text up on one left edge (about 6 to 8 % in) and keep the same margin at the edges.
- **The shot as the hero.** Make it big, let it bleed off an edge, and give it a slight turn or 3D angle with `shot`.
- **Use the page's own words and numbers** for headlines, stats and subtitles, and the host name as a quiet sign-off. Never invent a figure or a quote.
- **Check the preview** for collisions, orphans (a lone last word) and empty bands, then restyle with the `capture_id`.

## Craft notes

- **Outlined type** needs a static display font at a weight it has, such as Archivo Black, Anton or Bebas Neue with `weight: "regular"`. A variable font like Inter shows its overlapping contours when stroked, and a weight the font lacks is faked by drawing it twice.
- **Glows** are soft already; their full colour is only at the centre, so make them large (40 to 120 % of the width) and let them bleed off the canvas.
- **Light rays** are tall `polygon` triangles (`sides: 3`, the point at the top) with `colors` fading down them, blurred, turned and screen-blended. Rotation is about the layer's centre, so to fan rays from one light, put each centre half its length from the light along its angle: x = light.x − (height / 2) × sin(angle), y = light.y + (height / 2) × cos(angle) on a square canvas.
- **Stars, dust and sparkle** are one `pattern: "scatter"` layer (dots of varied size and brightness, `seed` to move them), with `fade` to thin them out.
- **A horizon or a rising planet** is a huge ellipse placed far below the canvas (positions go from -100 to 200 %) with a `ring` of the same size on it for the lit rim: one blurred and screen-blended, one crisp.
- **Text that flows:** a stack at one `anchor` keeps a badge, headline and sign-off apart however many lines the headline takes; fixed `y` positions collide when the words change.
- **`effects: { noise: 0 }`** turns off the editor's own noise when the art brings its grain.
- **No shot:** `mode: "none"` with a size makes a design from text, pictures, shapes and art alone.

Sizes are px at the canvas's own size (x_one_image is 2400×1350); positions are percents of the canvas.
